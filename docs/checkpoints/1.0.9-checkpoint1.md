# PhiStudio 1.0.9 checkpoint 1

Based on upstream `main` at `744243e1efac0031669a906cb025ebd7ea5e2857` (1.0.8).

## Micrology Playtest rendering

Micrology's first extended scale events extrapolate to large negative values before
their start. For example, the effective horizontal scales are approximately -1772
at 0:33 and -230 at 1:23. The line remains thin, but a fullscreen Canvas was receiving
rotated rectangles millions of pixels wide. A miss switches ordinary Playtest lines
to white, matching the reported white-flash symptom. The existing extreme-f32
clipper did not cover these intermediate scales.

The renderer now intersects oversized ordinary lines with the viewport in double
precision and submits a small convex path to Canvas. Clipping starts from the
viewport, preserving the line's thin dimension. Authored events, extrapolation,
judgement, color, opacity, thickness and the existing extreme-f32 compatibility path
are preserved. The shared renderer also uses bounded geometry for Preview, shader
layers and video export.

## Validation

- Synthetic post-miss Playtest regressions cover the two reported scales, 3:2 and
  16:9, and widths from 960 to 3840 pixels, including overlay and shader/export paths.
- Geometry tests compare point coverage against an independent inverse-transform
  oracle, including mirrored scales, rotation, offscreen lines, enclosing quads and
  the clipping threshold.
- An optional real-chart test renders 0:31–0:38 and 1:21–1:36 at 60 FPS in both
  aspects, at widths of 960 and 2400 pixels. Enable it with
  `PHISTUDIO_MICROLOGY_CHART=/path/to/Micrology.zip` when running the unit suite.
- ARM64 local validation uses `-I tools/termux-tests.gradle`. The excluded desktop
  native-graphics tests do not count as passed. Canvas recording tests verify draw
  commands and bounds; they do not emulate the phone GPU's rasterizer.

The final physical-device check is Playtest at 0:33–0:36 in 3:2 and 1:23–1:34 in
16:9, after a miss, as well as a direct start near each section and pause/retry.
No Android device was connected to ADB during local validation.

Build with `-PcheckpointBuild=true` for the separate `com.xpe.mobile.checkpoint` app.

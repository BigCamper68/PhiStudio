Phigros 4 noise field rendering reference

The shader programs are GLES2 adaptations of compiled GLSL ES 3 programs extracted
from Phigros 4.0.1. Their arithmetic, precision qualifiers and material parameters
are retained. These are compiled game shaders, not the original Unity ShaderLab
or the complete official game source code.

Published extraction, disassembly and textures:
https://github.com/CHCAT1320/CHCAT-Docs/tree/054fcfdddc4ddf66b1bbecc45df0d5264e3d3852/knowladge/phigros
Corrected texture exports and original hover sprite:
https://github.com/Phira-Pro/Phira-Pro/tree/b67e5e845b58e3cf89d674e782a9b3bd0f5e8af8/assets/blockarea

Game artwork and compiled shaders remain the work of Pigeon Games and their
respective authors. No ownership of the original game resources is claimed.
The Java integration, host validation adapter and extraction adapter are PhiStudio
code. Source hashes and pinned revisions are in sources.json.

The GLES adapter removes GLSL3 interface/location syntax, converts texture calls
to texture2D and maps scalar/RG render outputs into RGBA attachments. Explicit
LOD-zero sampling is equivalent on the bundled textures without mipmaps.
The game clock replaces Unity's global _Time so paused frames, seeking and video
export remain deterministic. All noise field event times remain in seconds.

Checkpoint 9's PhiraNoiseFieldMarkers adapter follows the Phira-Pro Line2Area
lifecycle and canvas transform implementation at
https://github.com/Phira-Pro/Phira-Pro/blob/abf652d2bbf1e04e6e5e0619edd4d5623a52634c/prpr/src/core/rpe_block.rs
